Letterfall

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Letterfall does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Data on your device

Your progress (stamps, stars, coins, streak and settings) is saved only on your iPhone. It is never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, Letterfall sends your scores and achievements to Apple's Game Center so they can appear on leaderboards. This is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not receive your Game Center details.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Children

Letterfall is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
